# End-User Manual & Operating Guide
## MoneyTrack – Personal Expense & Budget Management System

> **Document Version:** 1.0  
> **Audience:** End Users, Academic Evaluators  
> **Date:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 Logging In
1. Open your browser and navigate to **`http://localhost:3000`**.
2. If you don't have an account, click **"Create an account"** to register with your username, email, and password.
3. To explore with demo data, click the **"Use Demo Account"** button on the login screen (fills `demo@moneytrack.com` / `Demo@123`) and click **Sign In**.

---

## 2. Navigating MoneyTrack

### 2.1 The Dashboard
Upon logging in, the Executive Dashboard displays:
- **Financial KPI Cards:** Current Net Balance, Total Income this month, Total Expense this month, and Recorded Transaction count.
- **Cash Flow Overview:** A 12-month bar chart comparing income versus expenses.
- **Category Breakdown:** A donut chart showing the percentage distribution of your monthly expenses.
- **Quick Links:** Recent transactions, active budgets, and savings goals previews.

### 2.2 Managing Transactions
- **View Transactions:** Click **Transactions** in the sidebar.
- **Filter & Search:** Use the search bar to locate transactions by keyword, or filter by Type (Income/Expense) and Category.
- **Add Transaction:**
  1. Click **"+ Add Transaction"**.
  2. Toggle between **Expense** or **Income**.
  3. Enter the Amount, Category, Description, Date, and optional Notes.
  4. Click **Add Transaction**.
- **Edit / Delete:** Use the ✏️ (Edit) and 🗑️ (Delete) buttons on each row.

### 2.3 Category Management
- Navigate to **Categories** to view built-in system categories (🍔 Food, 🚗 Transport, 💰 Salary, etc.) and your custom categories.
- Click **"+ Add Custom Category"** to create a custom category with custom emoji icons and chart colors.

### 2.4 Setting Monthly Budgets
- Navigate to **Budgets** in the sidebar.
- Choose the Month and Year from the period selector.
- Click **"+ Set Budget"**, pick an expense category, and enter your maximum planned spending limit.
- Monitor the real-time progress bar:
  - 🟢 **Green (< 80%):** Spending is well within limits.
  - 🟡 **Yellow (80% - 100%):** Approaching budget limit.
  - 🔴 **Red (> 100%):** Over-budget alert showing the exact exceeded amount.

### 2.5 Tracking Savings Goals
- Navigate to **Savings** to track financial targets (e.g., Vacation Fund, New Laptop).
- Click **"Add Deposit Funds"** on any goal card to add money as you save. The progress bar and days remaining will update automatically.

### 2.6 Viewing Reports & Analytics
- Navigate to **Reports** to review deep analytics:
  - Savings Rate (%) for the selected month.
  - Visual pie charts and historical cash flow trends.
  - Category spending breakdown table showing count and percentage of total expenditure.

### 2.7 Profile & Preferences
- Navigate to **Profile** to update your display name, choose your default currency (`INR ₹`, `USD $`, `EUR €`, `GBP £`), or change your password.
