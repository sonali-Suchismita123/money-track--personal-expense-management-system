# Future Enhancements & Development Roadmap
## MoneyTrack – Personal Expense & Budget Management System

> **Document Version:** 1.0  
> **Date:** November 2026  
> **Milestone Plan:** Post-MVP / v2.0 Roadmap  

---

## 1. Product Roadmap (Version 2.0 & Beyond)

While MoneyTrack v1.0 delivers a full-stack personal expense and budget management solution, the following high-impact features are slated for future release phases:

---

### 1. Automated Transaction Ingestion & SMS Parsing
- **Android / PWA SMS Reader:** Automatically detect transactional SMS messages from Indian banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis) and UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
- **Auto-Categorization:** Parse merchant names (e.g. "Swiggy", "Uber", "Amazon") and automatically assign transactions to appropriate categories.

---

### 2. Recurring Transactions & Bill Reminders
- **Subscription Tracker:** Set up repeating income (monthly salary) or expenses (rent, Netflix, gym fees).
- **Upcoming Due Dates:** Calendar view and push notifications reminding users when bills are due.

---

### 3. Data Export & Financial Reports (PDF / Excel)
- **PDF Statement Generation:** Export formal monthly statements with charts, summary tables, and branding for tax or personal record-keeping.
- **CSV / Excel Export:** Download transaction logs with filters applied for external analysis in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

---

### 4. AI-Powered Financial Insights & Budgeting Advice
- **Predictive Spending:** Machine learning forecasts estimating month-end expenses based on daily run rates.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Alerts when an unusual spike in a category occurs compared to historical averages.
- **Personalized Recommendations:** Automated tips on areas where users can cut costs to reach their savings goals faster.

---

### 5. Multi-Account & Wallet Management
- **Account Segregation:** Ability to track balances across multiple bank accounts, cash wallets, and credit cards.
- **Account Transfers:** Log transfers between accounts (e.g., Bank Account to Cash) without counting them as expenses.
